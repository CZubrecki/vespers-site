Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

Vespers ("the App") is a Catholic Rosary and daily prayer app. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App keeps, what it sends, and the choices you have.

Your Prayer History and Settings

  • Prayer history (the Rosaries, Chaplets, and decades you pray, and the milestones you reach) is stored on your device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud database so it follows you to your other devices. Only you can read it; we cannot.
  • Preferences (reminder time, prayer text, beads side, prayers said, app icon) are stored on your device.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification content leaves it.

Anonymous Usage Analytics

To learn whether Vespers helps people pray, the App sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, our analytics provider. Each installation gets a random ID that is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID, or any account. Events describe moments in the App, such as starting a Rosary, finishing a decade, or choosing a reminder, along with:

  • Your App settings, such as the prayer text mode, text size, weekly goal, and whether reminders are on.
  • Coarse context: the liturgical season, the week the App was installed, the device type and system version, and whether accessibility features such as VoiceOver or larger text are on.
  • Lifetime totals kept with the random ID, such as how many decades and Rosaries have been prayed and the last day prayed.
  • Crash reports, so we can fix what breaks.

The App never sends anything you write, the exact minute of a reminder, your location, your contacts, or your photos. There is no advertising, no session recording, and no tracking across other apps or websites. IP addresses are discarded and not used to find your location.

You can turn this off at any time in More by switching off Share anonymous usage data. Once it is off, the App sends nothing more.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple iCloud stores your prayer history in your private iCloud database, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • PostHog (PostHog, Inc., United States) receives the anonymous usage events described above and processes them on our behalf. We do not sell or share this data with anyone else.

Internet Access

Vespers does not need an internet connection to pray. All prayers, scripture, and mysteries are bundled within the App. When a connection is available, it is used for iCloud sync and anonymous usage analytics.

Children's Privacy

Vespers does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Usage analytics are anonymous and can be turned off in More.

Data Deletion

Deleting the App removes the data on your device. Your prayer history in iCloud can be removed in your device's iCloud settings. Anonymous usage events are tied only to a random ID; deleting and reinstalling the App starts a new one, and you can ask us to delete the events from an installation through our support page.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via our support page.
